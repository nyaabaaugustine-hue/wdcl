# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

- Wiamoase diaspora and Ghanaians abroad — reconnecting, staying informed, looking for ways to support the community back home.
- Local community members and parents — finding clubs, facilities, location and hours.
- Partners, sponsors and volunteers — evaluating credibility before engaging.

All stakeholders are first-class; the site must serve the curious visitor and the committed supporter alike.

## Product Purpose

The public face of the Wiamoase Diaspora Public Library — a branch of the Ghana Library Authority built by Wiamoase citizens in the diaspora. It presents the library's story, facilities, clubs, news and sponsorship record, and makes it easy to visit, ask questions and get involved. Success: a visitor trusts the institution within seconds, finds what they need without friction, and leaves knowing how to support or participate.

## Brand Personality

Warm, rooted, welcoming.

Physical, not abstract: the feel of a well-kept community reading room — kente accents, hand-built shelves, people who know your name.

## Anti-references

- Corporate/sterile template look — stock blue, generic card grids, forgettable SaaS-landing feel.
- Over-decorated sites — heavy gradients, glassmorphism, flashy effects for their own sake.
- Bureaucratic/flat government pages — text dumps with no personality.

## Design Principles

1. Community truth over polish — never invent facts (hours, contacts, photos); honest "to be confirmed" states styled with care beat fabricated content.
2. Earned trust in seconds — credibility signals (Ghana Library Authority, diaspora-built story, named projects) surface early and clearly.
3. Warmth with restraint — kente/gold accents and photography carry the personality; decoration never crowds the content.
4. One accessible system — WCAG 2.1 AA is a floor, not a ceiling: keyboard, contrast and reduced-motion are part of every change.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA: ≥4.5:1 body contrast, full keyboard operability with visible focus, `prefers-reduced-motion` honored, semantic landmarks and labels, deep-link/topic prefill for assistive navigation, forms with programmatic error association.
